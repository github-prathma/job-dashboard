Referly

Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Referly ("the app") is a free, open-source dashboard for organizing your own job search. This page explains what it does with your information. The short version: your data stays in your own Google account and your own browser. The app has no server and no database, and we never see your data.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for one permission: "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (drive.file). With it, the app can:

  • create a Referly folder in your Google Drive with a tracker sheet, a search config document, your base resume and three folders;
  • read and update those files, and any tracker you choose yourself with Google's file picker;
  • remember which tracker is yours, using a private label Google attaches to that file for this app only.

The app cannot see, open or list any other file in your Google Drive. It does not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google service.

Where your data goes

  • Your browser talks directly to Google's servers. Your data is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored on GitHub, where the app's code is hosted.
  • When you paste a job link from Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby, your browser reads that public job posting directly from the job board to tailor a message. Only the posting's address is sent to them.
  • The app does not use analytics, ads or tracking cookies.

What is saved in your browser

Small conveniences are kept in your browser's local storage: your light or dark theme, which drafted messages you marked as sent, your sent-message log, and the job you last picked. Your Google sign-in is kept only for the current tab and expires after about an hour. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of this.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to show and update your own job search files for you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI models, and never read by people, except where you share it yourself.

Optional agents

If you choose to set up the job search agents, they run in your own Claude account as a scheduled task, using connectors you turn on yourself. That use is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy and your Claude settings, not by this app.

Removing your data

  • To stop the app's access, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete your data, delete the Referly folder from your Google Drive. The app keeps no copy.

Children

The app is meant for adults looking for work and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/github-prathma/job-dashboard/issues.
