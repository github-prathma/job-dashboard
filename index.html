    One-click drafting from LinkedIn

    No more copying names

    Draft with Job Agent
    1. Drag the button above to your browser's bookmarks bar (once).
    2. On a job here, click a Find people button such as Your connections.
    3. Open someone's LinkedIn profile and click Draft with Job Agent. This page opens with their name, profile, the job and the right message type filled in.

    What works

    Short, specific, one clear ask

    • Only recruiters get the work-authorization line (H-1B sponsorship); others don't, so the ask stays simple.
    • Connection notes must fit in 300 characters. Edit the draft if the counter turns red.
    • Paste the job link: the drafts then name skills from the posting that are also on your resume.
    • Send to a hiring manager or an engineer on the team before a recruiter when you want a referral.
